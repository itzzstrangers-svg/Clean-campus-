About Our Project

About

The Clean Campus Reporting Website helps students report cleanliness and maintenance issues such as garbage, water leakage, damaged benches, broken lights, and overflowing dustbins.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a cleaner, greener, and healthier campus by encouraging students to actively report problems and support a better learning environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a smart campus where cleanliness issues are resolved quickly through technology and student participation.
